### 3. CatVTON head-cutting + body-distortion fix (08d6a87 — the important one)
**Root cause found in the node source:** CatVTON's `CatVTON` node does
`resize_and_crop(target_image, (768,1024))` — a **center-crop to 3:4**. Photos
that aren't exactly 3:4 (e.g. 576×1246 tall, or 5312×2988 stored landscape w/
EXIF rotation) had their **head sliced off**, and EXIF-rotated photos were fed
**sideways** → distorted proportions (the "fatter" look).

**Fix:** `tryon._prep_person()` (in `tryon.py`) runs before every CatVTON call:
1. `ImageOps.exif_transpose` → portrait photos are upright.
2. **Letterbox** the full body onto the 768×1024 canvas (pad gray, **never crop**)
   → CatVTON's internal crop becomes a no-op. Head & feet always preserved.

Verified live on the tall 576×1246 photo: render now keeps the face. Regression
test in `tests/test_wardrobe.py` (`test_prep_person_preserves_head_and_exif`).

### 5. Auto-pick the best saved photo for a swap (v0.12.0)
- **Feature:** the Try-on page now auto-selects the best saved person photo for
  the current look (instead of always defaulting to the ★ default photo). Every
  saved photo is EXIF-aware scored server-side; the best is picked, the dropdown
  options show live scores, and a hint explains the pick (e.g. "✨ Auto-picked
  best saved photo for this look — Great (100/100) — good framing for a top").
- **Suitability heuristic** (`imageqa.suitability()`): reuses the existing
  person-QA heuristics (EXIF-aware aspect/full-body proxy, resolution, brightness,
  sharpness) plus a small per-category nudge — `top`/`outerwear` favor framing
  that keeps the face/upper body (bonus for w/h ∈ [0.55, 0.85], hard penalty for
  wide crops), `bottom`/`dress` favor full-body head-to-feet (bonus for tall
  portraits, hard penalty for wide crops). Landscape-stored/EXIF-rotated photos
  are auto-corrected before scoring (same fix as the CatVTON head fix).
- **One manual pick wins:** as soon as the user changes the saved-photo dropdown
  by hand, auto-picking stops for the rest of the session (no fighting the user).
- Endpoint: `GET /api/photos/suitability?category=top|bottom|dress|''` →
  `{category, best_id, best, ranked:[{...photo, score, grade, reason, size}]}`
  ranked best-first. Unreadable/corrupt photo files are skipped (never offered).
- Files: `app/imageqa.py` (suitability), `app/photos.py` (photos.suitability),
  `app/routes/photos_routes.py`, `static/js/tryon.js` + `templates/tryon.html`
  (auto-pick hint + scored dropdown).

## Current known limitations / next steps (open questions)
- **"Dress extends the dress"** — when the swap garment is clearly shorter than
  the person's pose/mask, CatVTON can stretch it. Under investigation: could try
  adjusting cloth_type (dress → overall), or the mask; not yet solved.
- **Photo guidance for the user:** for good CatVTON results provide frontal,
  full-body, well-lit, high-res, upright photos with neutral/simple clothing and
  arms slightly away from the body; avoid extreme crops, wide/long poses, and
  landscape-stored (EXIF-rotated) shots (now auto-corrected, but still best to
  provide proper portrait files). Not yet surfaced in the UI (could be a help
  panel on the Try-on page or per-category tips when the auto-pick hint shows).
