# Clueless Closet — getting the most out of it

*A plain-language guide for Melissa. No technical setup — just what to tap and why.*

> **The app link:** send her the same link / home-screen icon she already uses.

---

## 1. The one habit that matters most

When Cher suggests an outfit (the **Suggest** tab) she shows two buttons:

- **👍 Looks good** — you'd wear it.
- **👎 Not right** — you wouldn't. Then it asks **why**, and you tap the reason:
  *Not my style · Wrong color · Bad pattern/print · Too formal · Too casual ·
  Doesn't fit me · Don't like this item.*

**The reason is the part that teaches her.** Pick the honest one.

If it's really just the weather or your mood, tap **Just not today** — that one
counts as "no opinion" and won't be held against the clothes.

And don't worry about being negative: a 👎 fades away after about two weeks,
while a 👍 keeps working for a year. So be honest — it's the fastest way to get
suggestions you actually like.

## 2. Rate the clothes you love (and the ones you don't)

- **Wardrobe** tab → tap an item → **Rating (out of 10)**.
  7–10 = a favourite · 1–3 = you'd never wear it · 4–6 = no strong opinion.
- **Outfits** tab → open a look → same rating there. One rating on an outfit
  counts for every piece in it, so it's the quickest way to say a lot at once.

## 3. Fill in your Style profile once

**Account** tab → **Style profile**. This is the single biggest upgrade:

- **Never wear** — anything listed here is excluded completely (e.g. "shorts, yellow").
- **Least formal / most formal** — how dressed up you ever want to be.
- **Sizes** (top, bottom, shoes) — used when clothes are shared in the family.
- **Favourite colours / colours to avoid**, **style keywords** ("minimal, preppy…"),
  and **typical week** ("office 5×/week, gym 3×").
- **Temperature** — whether you usually run cold or hot.

## 4. Check what the app guessed about your clothes

When you add a garment the app fills the details in from the photo — and it is
sometimes confidently wrong. **Wardrobe** → tap the item and check, in this order:

1. **Category** (top / bottom / dress / …)
2. **Colour**
3. **Fit** — Regular, Baggy or Tight. **This one matters most**: it decides which
   photo of you gets used, so a wrong fit gives a strange-looking try-on.
4. Name / brand / size — nice to have, lower priority.

Also, if a card says **"⚠ looks similar to …"** it's a duplicate — keep the better photo.

## 5. Your photos

**Account** → **My photos**:

- Keep **2–3 full-body photos**: one in something fitted, one in something loose.
- **Type a short description on each** — "fitted jeans and a tucked-in top",
  "relaxed tee and baggy joggers". The app uses that to choose which photo fits
  which clothes.
- Mark one as **default**; if a photo shows a **red chip**, it isn't a good one to use.

## 6. Trying things on

**Try on** tab → pick a top / bottom / dress → **Try on**.

- One or two items take 1–2 minutes; three or more can take 3–4. Let it finish,
  and don't start a second one while it's working.
- If the clothes come out the wrong *shape* (trousers too long, tight things
  looking baggy) it's almost always because the photo of you is a different fit —
  switch to another photo of yourself.
- Want a small change? **Outfits** → open the look → **✨ Refine this outfit** and
  describe it ("make the sleeves longer", "turn her to the side", "change the light").
  It saves a **new** version and keeps the old one — nothing is ever overwritten.

## 7. Two things to know

- The "make a 3-second video clip" option is **switched off for now** — you won't
  see it, and that's expected.
- Everything you save stays. There's nothing you need to clean up or delete.

one thing you actually wore.
